A factory must rent warehouse space for the next 4 months. The required warehouse area and rental fees for different contract lengths are provided in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder. The factory can sign rental contracts of varying lengths, with longer contracts offering discounted rates. Each contract must cover consecutive months starting from the beginning of the period. Additional business rules include renting multiple warehouses in parallel, ensuring at least two different contract lengths are signed, mutual exclusion between 1-month and 4-month contracts, and limiting the number of distinct warehouse contracts to three. The demand for each month must be fully satisfied without shortage or excess. Decision variables include integer variables for the number of contracts signed and binary variables for logical conditions. The objective is to minimize the total rental cost over the 4 months.
