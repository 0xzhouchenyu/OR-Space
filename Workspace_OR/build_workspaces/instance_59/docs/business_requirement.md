A candy factory uses raw materials A, B, and C to process three different brands of candies: A, B, and C. The content of raw materials in each brand of candy, the cost of raw materials, the monthly limit of each raw material, and the unit processing fee and selling price of the three brands of candies are provided in the corresponding CSV files. Please refer to the data folder for specific parameter values. The objective is to determine the optimal production quantities of each candy brand to maximize the factory's profit.
