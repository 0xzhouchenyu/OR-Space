Jieli Company is finalizing staffing assignments for its two regional branches in Donghai City and Nanjiang City, where three types of professionals must be placed. The branch-by-branch demand for each specialty, together with each applicant’s specialty preference and city preference, is provided in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder. From an operating standpoint, the company cannot leave any required post uncovered, so the first commitment is that the full demand for all three specialties in both branches has to be met.

At the same time, the HR team has been asked to follow an updated placement policy. After all required positions are filled, the next non-negotiable expectation is that at least the specified target number of recruited personnel are placed into their preferred specialty. Once those two commitments are secured, management wants placements to reflect overall match quality more strongly than before. In particular, they want to place as many recruits as possible in assignments that satisfy both dimensions of preference at once: the person works in their preferred specialty and is also posted to their preferred city.

Please use the applicant preference information and branch demand data in the CSV files in the data folder to determine the best personnel arrangement under this policy, and report the maximum possible number of recruits who can simultaneously receive both their preferred specialty and their preferred city.
