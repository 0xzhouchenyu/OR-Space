The blending plant is still working with the same four liquid raw materials, A through D, and operations should continue to treat them as fully miscible with no yield loss in mixing. What has changed is the commercial offering: each of the two product families is now sold through two contract channels, premium and standard. The premium channel exists because several customers are willing to pay more for tighter sulfur performance, while the standard channel keeps the legacy specification and price position. In practice, the monthly plan therefore has to decide how much of each raw material is directed into A-type premium, A-type standard, B-type premium, and B-type standard output, recognizing that every ton of input becomes one ton available for sale in one of those four finished streams.

Quality compliance has to be handled at the contract-channel level, not just at the broader product-family level. Sulfur data for each raw material remain in `table_1.csv` under `Sulfur_Content_Percentage`, and the blended sulfur result for each of the four saleable streams must stay within its own contractual ceiling. The two premium streams are governed by `max_sulfur_content_A_premium` and `max_sulfur_content_B_premium`; the two standard streams follow `max_sulfur_content_A_standard` and `max_sulfur_content_B_standard`. Because these are blend specifications, compliance depends on the sulfur carried by the raw materials actually assigned to each stream relative to the tons shipped in that same stream; if a stream is not produced, there is nothing to certify for that channel. Material D also continues to be the constrained feedstock, so total usage of D across all four streams cannot go beyond `max_supply_D` tons.

Sales planning remains anchored at the product-family level even though contracting is now tiered. That means the combined premium and standard tonnage sold for family A must fit within `market_demand_A`, and the combined premium and standard tonnage sold for family B must fit within `market_demand_B`. At the same time, the commercial team has negotiated minimum premium participation and also set exposure caps so that the plant is not pushed too far into the tighter sulfur commitments. As a result, premium tons for family A must land within the range defined by `min_premium_share_A` and `max_premium_share_A`, and premium tons for family B must land within the range defined by `min_premium_share_B` and `max_premium_share_B`.

The economic target is still to deliver the most profitable feasible production plan. Revenue must now reflect the four distinct selling terms listed in `general_parameters.csv`: `selling_price_A_premium`, `selling_price_A_standard`, `selling_price_B_premium`, and `selling_price_B_standard`, all in thousand yuan per ton. Raw material purchase prices remain in `table_1.csv`, also in thousand yuan per ton, and should be charged against whatever quantities of A, B, C, and D are consumed in the four finished streams. Please use the CSV files in the data folder for all numeric values when determining the best blending and sales mix under these supply, demand, premium-channel, and sulfur-compliance requirements.
