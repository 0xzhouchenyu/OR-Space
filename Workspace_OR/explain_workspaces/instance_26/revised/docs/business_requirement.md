The store is reviewing its daily staffing plan for the 24-hour operation because traffic now swings much more sharply by time of day. Management wants to keep enough experienced people on the floor at all times, but it also wants the flexibility to handle the midday rush without paying for unnecessary full-time coverage in quieter shoulder periods. The day is divided into six consecutive 4-hour periods, indexed 0 through 5: period 0 is 2:00–6:00, period 1 is 6:00–10:00, period 2 is 10:00–14:00, period 3 is 14:00–18:00, period 4 is 18:00–22:00, and period 5 is 22:00–2:00. In each period j, the number shown as `Required_Salespeople[j]` in table_1.csv must be physically present and working for that entire 4-hour block.

The staffing plan now uses two kinds of labor. Full-time staff remain the core crew: anyone starting in shift i works `shift_duration` = 8 consecutive hours, beginning at the start times listed in `shift_start_times` = 2:00;6:00;10:00;14:00;18:00;22:00, so a full-time person who starts in period i is on duty for period i and period (i+1) mod 6. Each such full-time assignment carries a daily cost of `full_time_cost`. Alongside them, the store has launched a part-time program for short, demand-driven coverage. A part-time person assigned to period j works exactly that single 4-hour period and no other, and each such assignment carries a daily cost of `part_time_cost`.

Because part-time labor is meant to absorb peaks rather than replace the core team, there are two operating rules around its use. First, in any period j, the combined people on the floor from the two overlapping full-time starts and the part-time workers assigned to that same period must be enough to cover `Required_Salespeople[j]`. Second, reliance on part-time labor is capped for training, supervision, and HR-management reasons: no more than `part_time_cap_per_period` part-time workers may be used in any one period. At the same time, store leadership wants continuity, product knowledge, and supervisory presence on every shift, so the number of full-time staff physically present in each period—coming from the full-time starts in period j and period (j-1) mod 6—must never fall below `min_full_time_per_period`.

With those operating expectations in place, the staffing plan should be chosen to deliver the required coverage at the lowest total daily labor cost across both labor types.
