A steel pipe retailer purchases raw pipes from a steel mill and cuts them according to customer requirements before resale. Each raw steel pipe supplied by the mill has a specific length. A customer has placed an order for pipes of various lengths and quantities. To simplify the production process, the number of cutting patterns to be used is limited. Additional costs are incurred depending on the usage frequency of each cutting pattern, with varying rates based on the frequency ranking. Furthermore, under each cutting pattern, there is a restriction on the maximum number of cuts per raw pipe, and leftover material waste is limited. The objective is to determine the cutting plan (selection of patterns and allocation of raw pipes) that minimizes the total cost. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
