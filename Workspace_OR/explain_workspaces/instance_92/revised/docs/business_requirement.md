A school is organizing a trip for a group of students. The transportation company has buses and minibuses available, but there is a limited number of drivers. The goal is to determine how many of each type of vehicle should be used to minimize the transportation cost. Due to a new safety policy, for every minibus rented, at least two buses must be rented to act as escorts. Additionally, the school requires at least one minibus to be rented. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
