The computer lab is reviewing its weekly staffing plan for the Monday-to-Friday operating window from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Throughout those hours, the help desk must remain continuously staffed, so each day needs exactly 14 hours of student coverage in total, with exactly one student on duty at any moment. Students 1-4 are undergraduates and students 5-6 are graduate students. Their hourly wages, along with the maximum number of hours each person is available on each day, are listed in `table_1.csv`.

The lab still needs to respect the working rules already in place for student scheduling. Each undergraduate is expected to contribute at least `min_undergrad_hours` = 8 hours during the week, and each graduate student must contribute at least `min_grad_hours` = 7 hours during the week. To keep schedules manageable alongside coursework, no student may be brought in on more than `max_shifts_per_student` = 2 days in the week, and the lab does not want more than `max_students_per_day` = 3 different students sharing coverage on any one day. Whenever a student is assigned time on a given day, that assignment must fit within that student's listed daily availability.

There are also two practical policy updates that now matter financially and operationally. First, after a recent safety review, the department wants stronger graduate-student oversight in the lab, especially for escalations and equipment handling, so graduate students together must cover at least `min_grad_weekly_coverage_hours` = 16 hours across the week. Second, every time a student is scheduled on a particular day, the lab incurs a fixed opening and setup cost of `daily_open_fee_cny` = 12 CNY for that student-day, on top of the hourly wage; this is tracked using `z[i,d]`, which matches whether that student is scheduled that day through `y[i,d]`.

The planning task is therefore to arrange the weekly schedule so that all required desk coverage is provided at the lowest possible total weekly cost, counting both hourly wages and the 12 CNY fixed charge for every `(student, day)` worked.
