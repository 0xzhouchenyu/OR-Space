A university computer lab employs undergraduate and graduate students to answer questions during operating hours. The lab operates from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and exactly one student must be on duty at all times during these hours. Each undergraduate must work a minimum number of hours per week, and each graduate student must also meet a weekly minimum. Additional constraints include limits on the number of shifts each student can work per week and the maximum number of students scheduled per day. The goal is to establish a mathematical model to minimize the total wages paid. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
