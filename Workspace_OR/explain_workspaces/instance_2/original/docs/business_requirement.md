A fighter jet is a crucial combat tool, but to make it effective, enough trained pilots are required. Therefore, some fighter jets each year must be allocated exclusively for pilot training. The annual production of fighter jets is determined for two years, and each training jet can train a certain number of pilots per year. Each trained pilot can operate one combat jet in subsequent years. Training starts in year 1 and continues for 2 years. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
