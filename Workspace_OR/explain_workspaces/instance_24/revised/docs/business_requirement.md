A manufacturing company needs to transport products from the warehouse to three different sales points. The company has several transportation options, but due to energy consumption constraints, certain combinations of vehicles cannot be used simultaneously. Each transportation option generates pollution, and the total pollution generated from all trips is subject to a maximum limit. Additionally, there are minimum requirements for the number of trips using specific vehicles. Each transportation option has a specific capacity per trip. The goal is to plan the transportation to ensure all products are delivered while minimizing the total pollution generated. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
