The Li family is planning a trip and must decide which children to take. They can take up to three children, and they have decided to take at least two. Bob, the youngest child, will definitely be included in the trip. There are constraints based on relationships among the children: Alice and Diana cannot both be taken, Bob and Charlie cannot both be taken, Charlie must be accompanied by Diana, and Diana must be accompanied by Ella.
