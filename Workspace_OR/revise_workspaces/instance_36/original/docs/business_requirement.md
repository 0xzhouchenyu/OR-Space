The Vehicle Routing Problem with Hard Time Windows (VRPHTW) involves optimizing delivery routes for a logistics provider operating in a city's central business district (CBD). The problem includes constraints such as vehicle capacity, customer demands, strict time windows, and fixed service durations. The objective is to minimize the total distance traveled by all vehicles while meeting all constraints. Specific details about the depot, customer locations, demands, time windows, and service durations can be found in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder.
