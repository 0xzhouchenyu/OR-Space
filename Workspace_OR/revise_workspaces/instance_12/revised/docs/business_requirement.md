Red Star Plastics Factory produces six distinct types of plastic containers. Each container type has specific characteristics such as volume, market demand, and unit variable production cost. The production of any container type requires specialized equipment, and activating the equipment incurs a fixed setup cost. If the production quantity of a container type is insufficient to meet its demand, larger or equal volume containers can be used as substitutes. The objective is to develop a production plan that minimizes the total cost, including variable production costs and fixed setup costs, while ensuring all demand is met. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
