We are reviewing how factories should be positioned across the available locations for outbound handling, with the aim of keeping total transportation and handling spend as low as possible. The transportation volumes from each factory to all destination locations, together with the unit transportation costs between locations, are provided in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder. Each factory i must be placed at exactly one location p, and no location should host more than one factory. Once a factory is placed, all of its outbound volume `outbound_volume_i = sum_j d_ij` is to be processed through that assigned location.

This planning change comes from a service-level split in our logistics network. For any factory-location pairing `(i,p)`, operations may be run in either standard service or premium service. Standard service reflects the usual handling pattern at that location. Premium service is available where needed to protect tighter shipping commitments, but it comes at a higher rate. The standard unit cost for factory i operating from location p is `c_std_ip = (sum_j d_ij * c_pj) / outbound_volume_i` when `outbound_volume_i > 0`, and `0` otherwise. Premium service at the same factory-location pairing is charged at `c_prem_ip = premium_cost_multiplier * c_std_ip`, with `premium_cost_multiplier = 1.5`, so premium handling is 50% more expensive per unit than standard handling.

Because premium handling is intended for committed higher-service flows rather than token usage, if premium is selected for a factory at its assigned location, it must cover a meaningful share of that factory’s outbound business. Specifically, whenever `y_i_p` indicates premium handling for `(i,p)`, the volume handled in premium mode `v_prem_i_p` must be at least `min_premium_share * outbound_volume_i`, where `min_premium_share = 0.4`. In other words, at least 40% of that factory’s total outbound volume must move under premium service if premium is chosen. The factory’s total outbound volume is split between `v_std_i_p` and `v_prem_i_p`, and that split can occur only at the location actually assigned to the factory; premium volume is possible only where premium has been selected for that assigned location. The relevant scale for each factory is `M_i = outbound_volume_i`.

The cost we care about is the full handling and transportation cost after that service choice is made: standard volume `v_std_i_p` is charged at `c_std_ip`, and premium volume `v_prem_i_p` is charged at `c_prem_ip`. Please use the corresponding CSV files in the data folder, along with `premium_cost_multiplier` and `min_premium_share` from `general_parameters.csv`, to determine the factory-to-location arrangement and associated service selections that deliver the lowest total cost.
