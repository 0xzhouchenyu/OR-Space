A company is producing two products, X and Y, measured in standardized batches. The weekly production plan may involve fractional batches when appropriate. Resources required for production are divided into machine time for automated processing and craftsman time for manual finishing. The company has limited availability of machine time and craftsman time during the next working week. Costs are incurred for machine time and craftsman time, while idle time incurs no cost. Revenue is generated for each batch of products X and Y sold. Additionally, there is a contractual obligation to produce a minimum number of batches of product X each week.
