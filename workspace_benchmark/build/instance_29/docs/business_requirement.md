Jieli Company needs to recruit three types of professionals to work in the two regional branches located in Donghai City and Nanjiang City. The demand for different professionals in these regional branches, as well as the specialties and preferences of applicants, are detailed in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder. The company's personnel arrangement considers the following three priorities: Priority 1: All three types of professionals needed are fully met; Priority 2: A specific number of recruited personnel meet their preferred specialty; Priority 3: A specific number of recruited personnel meet their preferred city. Try to establish a mathematical model for goal planning accordingly, and answer the question: At least how many people can't meet Priority 3 under this priority?
