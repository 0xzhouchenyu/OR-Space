A company is evaluating whether to hire candidates to join their R&D team. The goal is to minimize the total salary paid to the selected candidates while adhering to budget constraints and meeting skill level and project management experience requirements. The company has specific constraints on the number of employees they can hire, the total skill level, and the total project management experience of the team.
