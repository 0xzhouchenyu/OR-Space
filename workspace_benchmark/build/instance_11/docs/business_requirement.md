A company produces two types of small motorcycles. Type A is entirely manufactured in-house, while Type B is assembled from imported parts. The time required per unit for manufacturing, assembly, and inspection, as well as the weekly available capacity, hourly costs, and selling prices, are provided in the corresponding CSV files. Decision variables represent the weekly production quantities of Type A and Type B. The company's operational requirements include a minimum weekly profit, a minimum production quantity for Type A, and minimizing idle time for each process proportional to hourly costs. Please refer to the CSV files for specific parameter values.
