Tom and Jerry want this season’s acreage plan for corn, wheat, soybeans, and sorghum to reflect how the Sunshine Valley farm will actually be run in the field, not just what looks best on paper. Profit is still the priority, using the same yields, selling prices, variable costs, land availability, and any other operating data already provided for the farm.

Two practical commitments now shape the crop mix. First, the farm’s agronomy setup for its most input-intensive row crop has to be locked in before planting: the same specialized planter configuration, irrigation preparation, and support equipment are needed for both corn and soybeans, and the crew can only prepare one of those two programs for the season. Once that commitment is made, the other crop is off the table for this year’s planting plan. Second, Tom and Jerry are not free to skip corn entirely, because a standing outlet agreement calls for a minimum of 20 acres of corn to be grown.

Taken together, these season-start decisions mean the best plan must still be chosen on a profit basis, but only among acreage mixes that respect that operational commitment and the 20-acre corn obligation. Please use the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for all specific parameter values.
