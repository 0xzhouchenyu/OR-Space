A factory produces three types of products: I, II, and III. Each product must undergo two processing stages, A and B. The factory has two types of equipment to complete stage A and three types of equipment to complete stage B. The production rules specify which equipment can process each product. The objective is to determine the optimal production plan to maximize the factory's total profit. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
