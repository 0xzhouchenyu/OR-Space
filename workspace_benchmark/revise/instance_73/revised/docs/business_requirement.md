The Li family is no longer planning a one-day outing; they are scheduling a two-day trip and need a day-by-day attendance plan for Alice, Bob, Charlie, Diana, and Ella. The parents want each day to remain manageable, so the headcount on day 1 and again on day 2 must stay within the family’s stated lower and upper limits, `min_children_trip` and `max_children_trip`. They also want to avoid turning the trip into a rotating cast across the weekend, so across both days combined, the number of different children who appear at least once cannot exceed `max_distinct_children_trip`. Bob is still non-negotiable from a parenting standpoint, but in this two-day version that requirement is tracked through `min_bob_days`, meaning he must join often enough to satisfy that minimum over the two scheduled days.

The family is also putting a tighter structure around cost. Each child carries a per-day trip expense listed in `table_1.csv` under `Cost[child]`, and that charge applies on every day that child travels. However, the parents expect some efficiency when the same child is part of both days of the plan, so the trip receives a fixed savings amount of `bonus_saving` for each child whose attendance is continuous across the full two-day outing. Even with that repeat-attendance savings, the full plan still has to fit within `total_cost_budget`, and the preferred plan is the one that leaves the family with the lowest net two-day spend after applying all eligible savings.

A few family logistics and supervision rules still govern who can go, and they now matter separately for each day rather than just for the trip as a whole. Alice and Diana are not to be scheduled together on the same day. Bob and Charlie are also not to be paired on the same day. Charlie is only allowed to join when Diana is already part of that day’s group, and whenever Diana is included, Ella must be with her as well. On top of that, school and personal commitments narrow availability further: Charlie can participate only on day 2, Diana can participate only on day 2, and Alice can participate only on day 1.

Use these planning rules to determine the day 1 and day 2 participation list. The numerical values for `Cost[child]` are in `table_1.csv`, and the trip-wide settings `min_children_trip`, `max_children_trip`, `max_distinct_children_trip`, `total_cost_budget`, `bonus_saving`, and `min_bob_days` are provided in `general_parameters.csv` in the data folder.
