A company is planning to recruit new employees for its team. They aim to minimize the total salary paid to the selected candidates while adhering to several constraints. The team will hire a maximum of three new employees and ensure the total payment does not exceed the budget. At least one candidate with a Master's or Doctoral degree will be selected. The total work experience of the selected candidates must be no less than a specified threshold. Additionally, due to equivalent professional skills, the company will choose at most one candidate from a specific pair. Finally, they will hire at least two new employees.
