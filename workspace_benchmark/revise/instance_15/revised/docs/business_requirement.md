The toolroom plan now has to reflect two new realities that were not part of the earlier operating brief. First, the repair area is working under fixed per-stage throughput commitments because the expedited bench and the standard bench each have their own staffing and equipment ceiling. In practical terms, no stage may send more tools to the fast-return route than `max_fast_stage`, and no stage may send more to the slow-return route than `max_slow_stage`. Second, the plant’s ESG review has put this tool family under a single planning-horizon emissions allowance, so the full plan must stay within `carbon_budget` after counting the applicable `emission_factors` for every newly purchased tool, every repair action, and any serviceable tools deliberately brought into the horizon before stage 1.

Operations also wants the planning team to recognise that the horizon does not have to begin empty on the ready-to-use side. If it is commercially sensible and still consistent with the carbon allowance, the factory may secure an initial bank of usable tools before the first stage starts. At the opening of the horizon there is no inherited backlog of dirty tools waiting for repair, but there may be this upfront clean stock available immediately for deployment. After that, the same physical logic still applies: a stage can only draw from tools already on hand in usable condition, from new purchases, and from repairs whose turnaround has fully elapsed according to `fast_repair_duration` or `slow_repair_duration`; once a tool is used in a stage, it leaves the clean pool and joins the dirty stream until it is assigned to one of the two repair channels or left to wait for a later stage.

The service expectation has also been softened in a controlled way. Each stage still carries its stated `tool_requirement[t]`, but management acknowledges that, under the combined pressure of repair bottlenecks and the emissions cap, there may be cases where the requirement cannot be completely covered on time. Any such miss is allowed only if it is recorded explicitly and charged through `penalty_shortage`, so the plan weighs the business cost of falling short against buying more tools, using the faster repair route, using the slower repair route, or arranging more initial clean stock before stage 1. The planning objective remains to find the lowest-cost operating plan across purchases, repair spending, and any shortage penalties, while respecting repair timing, stage repair capacities, and the overall carbon limit.
