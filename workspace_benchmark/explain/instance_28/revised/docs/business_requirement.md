An individual is planning how to allocate a pool of funds across several available investment options over the next three years, with the aim of finishing the third year with as much money as possible. The available opportunities, timing, and financial terms are provided in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder.

One practical wrinkle is that the special investment channels are not costless to access. Whenever the investor chooses to use one of these special investments at the start of a year, the provider charges a fixed management fee for setting up and administering that placement. From a planning standpoint, those fees need to be treated as real cash costs associated with making use of the special option in that year, and they reduce what the investor ultimately keeps. As a result, the right measure of success here is not just gross principal plus interest, but the total net principal and interest remaining at the end of the third year after deducting all such fixed management fees triggered by using the special investments.

Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for the specific parameter values and determine the investment plan that gives the highest end-of-year-3 net total.
