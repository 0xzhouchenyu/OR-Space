A timber storage and transport company operates a warehouse for storing and transporting timber for sale. Due to seasonal price fluctuations, the company purchases timber at the beginning of each quarter, with part of it being sold within the quarter and part being stored for future sales. The company has constraints on storage capacity, storage costs, and sales volumes. Additionally, timber must be sold by the end of autumn due to its unsuitability for long-term storage. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for specific parameter values.
