An Italian transportation company needs to move empty containers from its warehouses to major national ports. The container inventory at the warehouses, the demand at the ports, the cost per kilometer, truck capacity, and distances between locations are provided in the corresponding CSV files in the data folder. Please refer to these files for specific parameter values. The task is to write a mathematical program to find the minimum cost transportation policy and solve it using COPTPY.

Revision brief from the business owner:

In the stakeholder handoff for this case, the planning lead framed the change in business terms rather than as a spreadsheet checklist. Finance is asking for the same physical plan to be read under a different accounting story. The important issue is where the money belongs in the operating ledger, not simply whether the number appears somewhere. The port plan now treats shortage as a staged operating incident. Each uncovered container still carries the port’s daily disruption charge, but once a port shortage rises above its local grace band, the recovery desk must be opened for that port. The Adriatic ports also share a regional recovery desk if their combined uncovered demand goes above `adriatic_pool_shortage_trigger`. Transport spend and all recovery charges are weighed together.
