\section*{Sets and Indices}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Products \(P = \{\mathrm{M}, \mathrm{W}\}\), where
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \(\mathrm{M}\): microwave oven,
        \item \(\mathrm{W}\): water heater.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section*{Parameters}

For each product \(p \in P\):

\begin{itemize}
    \item \(a_p\): hours required in workshop A per unit of product \(p\)
    (code fields: \texttt{products[p]['a\_hrs']}).
    \item \(b_p\): hours required in workshop B per unit of product \(p\)
    (code fields: \texttt{products[p]['b\_hrs']}).
    \item \(c_p\): inspection and sales cost per unit of product \(p\)
    (code fields: \texttt{products[p]['insp\_cost']}).
\end{itemize}

Plant-wide parameters:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \(H_A\): available monthly hours in workshop A
    (code: \texttt{params['workshop\_a\_available\_hours']}).
    \item \(H_B\): available monthly hours in workshop B
    (code: \texttt{params['workshop\_b\_available\_hours']}).
    \item \(O_A\): overtime limit in workshop A
    (code: \texttt{params['workshop\_a\_overtime\_limit']}).
    \item \(r_A\): hourly cost in workshop A
    (code: \texttt{params['workshop\_a\_hourly\_cost']}).
    \item \(r_B\): hourly cost in workshop B
    (code: \texttt{params['workshop\_b\_hourly\_cost']}).
    \item \(C\): monthly limit on inspection and sales cost
    (code: \texttt{params['inspection\_sales\_cost\_limit']}).
    \item \(L_{\mathrm{M}}\): minimum monthly sales requirement for microwave ovens
    (code: \texttt{params['microwave\_minimum\_sales']}).
    \item \(L_{\mathrm{W}}\): minimum monthly sales requirement for water heaters
    (code: \texttt{params['water\_heater\_minimum\_sales']}).
\end{itemize}

The code defines per-unit objective coefficients:
\[
\alpha_{\mathrm{M}} = a_{\mathrm{M}} r_A + b_{\mathrm{M}} r_B + c_{\mathrm{M}},
\qquad
\alpha_{\mathrm{W}} = a_{\mathrm{W}} r_A + b_{\mathrm{W}} r_B + c_{\mathrm{W}},
\]
(code: \texttt{rev\_m}, \texttt{rev\_w}).

\section*{Decision Variables}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \(x_{\mathrm{M}}\) (code: \texttt{x1}): production/sales quantity of microwave ovens.
    \item \(x_{\mathrm{W}}\) (code: \texttt{x2}): production/sales quantity of water heaters.
\end{itemize}

Both variables are continuous in the implementation, with lower bounds:
\[
x_{\mathrm{M}} \ge L_{\mathrm{M}}, \qquad x_{\mathrm{W}} \ge L_{\mathrm{W}}.
\]

\section*{Objective}

The implemented model maximizes
\[
\max \; \alpha_{\mathrm{M}} x_{\mathrm{M}} + \alpha_{\mathrm{W}} x_{\mathrm{W}},
\]
i.e.,
\[
\max \; (a_{\mathrm{M}} r_A + b_{\mathrm{M}} r_B + c_{\mathrm{M}}) x_{\mathrm{M}}
      + (a_{\mathrm{W}} r_A + b_{\mathrm{W}} r_B + c_{\mathrm{W}}) x_{\mathrm{W}}.
\]

\section*{Constraints}

Workshop A capacity with overtime:
\[
a_{\mathrm{M}} x_{\mathrm{M}} + a_{\mathrm{W}} x_{\mathrm{W}} \le H_A + O_A.
\]

Workshop B utilization lower bound:
\[
b_{\mathrm{M}} x_{\mathrm{M}} + b_{\mathrm{W}} x_{\mathrm{W}} \ge H_B.
\]

Inspection and sales cost limit:
\[
c_{\mathrm{M}} x_{\mathrm{M}} + c_{\mathrm{W}} x_{\mathrm{W}} \le C.
\]

Minimum sales requirements, implemented as variable lower bounds:
\[
x_{\mathrm{M}} \ge L_{\mathrm{M}},
\qquad
x_{\mathrm{W}} \ge L_{\mathrm{W}}.
\]

\section*{Notes on Implementation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The model is a linear program solved by PuLP through \texttt{GUROBI\_CMD}.
    \item The quantities \(x_{\mathrm{M}}\) and \(x_{\mathrm{W}}\) are not declared integer; they are continuous nonnegative variables with the stated lower bounds.
    \item The code reads monthly sales estimates from the data file, but these estimates are not used in the optimization model.
    \item The objective coefficients are constructed directly from workshop hourly costs plus inspection/sales cost per unit. Thus the implemented objective is exactly the maximization of \(\alpha_{\mathrm{M}} x_{\mathrm{M}} + \alpha_{\mathrm{W}} x_{\mathrm{W}}\), not profit or revenue net of costs unless that interpretation is imposed externally.
    \item The workshop B condition is implemented as a lower bound (\(\ge H_B\)) on utilized hours, not as an upper-capacity restriction.
\end{itemize}
