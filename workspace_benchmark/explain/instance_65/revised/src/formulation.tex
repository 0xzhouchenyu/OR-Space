\section*{Sets and Indices}

\[
P=\{m,w\}
\]

where
\[
m=\text{microwave ovens}, \qquad w=\text{water heaters}.
\]

\section*{Parameters}

For each product \(p\in P\):

\[
a_p = \text{Workshop A hours per unit},
\qquad
b_p = \text{Workshop B hours per unit},
\qquad
c_p = \text{inspection/sales cost per unit}.
\]

From \texttt{table\_1.csv},
\[
a_m=\texttt{Workshop\_A\_Hours for Microwave\_Oven}, \quad
a_w=\texttt{Workshop\_A\_Hours for Water\_Heater},
\]
\[
b_m=\texttt{Workshop\_B\_Hours for Microwave\_Oven}, \quad
b_w=\texttt{Workshop\_B\_Hours for Water\_Heater},
\]
\[
c_m=\texttt{Inspection\_Sales\_Cost for Microwave\_Oven}, \quad
c_w=\texttt{Inspection\_Sales\_Cost for Water\_Heater}.
\]

General parameters from \texttt{general\_parameters.csv}:

\[
H_A=\texttt{workshop\_a\_available\_hours}, \qquad
H_B=\texttt{workshop\_b\_available\_hours},
\]
\[
K_A=\texttt{workshop\_a\_hourly\_cost}, \qquad
K_B=\texttt{workshop\_b\_hourly\_cost},
\]
\[
L=\texttt{inspection\_sales\_cost\_limit},
\]
\[
\underline{x}_m=\texttt{microwave\_minimum\_sales}, \qquad
\underline{x}_w=\texttt{water\_heater\_minimum\_sales},
\]
\[
O_A=\texttt{workshop\_a\_overtime\_limit},
\qquad
S_A=\texttt{setup\_hours\_A},
\]
\[
T=\texttt{tech\_pool\_hours},
\qquad
\rho_m=\texttt{tech\_rate\_m},
\qquad
\rho_w=\texttt{tech\_rate\_w},
\]
\[
g_m=\texttt{green\_bonus\_m}, \qquad
g_w=\texttt{green\_bonus\_w},
\]
\[
M=\texttt{bigM}.
\]

The code constructs per-unit objective coefficients as
\[
r_m = a_m K_A + b_m K_B + c_m,
\qquad
r_w = a_w K_A + b_w K_B + c_w.
\]

\section*{Decision Variables}

\[
x_m \ge \underline{x}_m
\qquad
\text{(code: \texttt{x\_m})}
\]
number of microwave ovens produced/sold,

\[
x_w \ge \underline{x}_w
\qquad
\text{(code: \texttt{x\_w})}
\]
number of water heaters produced/sold,

\[
z_m \in \{0,1\}
\qquad
\text{(code: \texttt{z\_m})}
\]
activation indicator for microwave use of workshop A setup gate,

\[
z_w \in \{0,1\}
\qquad
\text{(code: \texttt{z\_w})}
\]
activation indicator for water-heater use of workshop A setup gate.

\section*{Objective}

The implemented model maximizes
\[
\max \; (r_m+g_m)x_m + (r_w+g_w)x_w.
\]

Equivalently, after substitution of the constructed coefficients,
\[
\max \; (a_m K_A+b_m K_B+c_m+g_m)x_m
      + (a_w K_A+b_w K_B+c_w+g_w)x_w.
\]

\section*{Constraints}

Workshop A capacity with overtime and fixed setup consumption for each activated product:
\[
a_m x_m + a_w x_w + S_A(z_m+z_w) \le H_A + O_A.
\]

Workshop B utilization lower bound:
\[
b_m x_m + b_w x_w \ge H_B.
\]

Inspection and sales cost budget:
\[
c_m x_m + c_w x_w \le L.
\]

Big-\(M\) gate linking microwave production to activation:
\[
x_m \le M z_m.
\]

Big-\(M\) gate linking water-heater production to activation:
\[
x_w \le M z_w.
\]

Shared certified-technician pool:
\[
\rho_m x_m + \rho_w x_w \le T.
\]

At least one activation variable must be on:
\[
z_m + z_w \ge 1.
\]

\section*{Notes on Implementation}

The formulation above matches the model created in \texttt{current\_heuristic.py} without correction or reinterpretation.

In particular:

\begin{itemize}
\item The objective coefficients \(r_m,r_w\) are not external selling prices; they are computed in code from workshop-hour coefficients and inspection/sales cost, then the green bonuses are added.
\item The workshop A certification rule is implemented as a fixed setup-hour charge \(S_A\) for each product with \(z_p=1\), via \(S_A(z_m+z_w)\).
\item The green bonuses \(g_m,g_w\) are applied to all units \(x_m,x_w\) in the objective, not only beyond a threshold.
\item The activation logic is one-sided: \(x_p \le M z_p\). Because \(x_m\) and \(x_w\) already have positive lower bounds \(\underline{x}_m,\underline{x}_w\), feasibility forces \(z_m=z_w=1\) in this instance, making the additional condition \(z_m+z_w\ge 1\) redundant in practice, though it is present in the code.
\item Workshop B is modeled with a \emph{minimum} usage requirement \(b_m x_m+b_w x_w \ge H_B\), not an upper-capacity limit.
\item The model is solved as a mixed-integer linear program with Gurobi through \texttt{GUROBI\_CMD(msg=0)}.
\end{itemize}
