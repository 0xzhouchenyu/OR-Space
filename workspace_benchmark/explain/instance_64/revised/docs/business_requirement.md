The company is reviewing how to staff its R&D team for the next hiring cycle and still needs to keep total hiring cost under control while meeting the team’s operating requirements. The candidate list, including each person’s `Salary`, `Skill_Level`, and `Project_Management_Experience`, is provided in `data/table_1.csv` for candidates F, G, H, I, and J. We still need to stay within `company_budget`, keep the team size within `max_employees`, reach at least `min_skill_level` in total technical capability, and reach at least `min_project_experience` in total project management experience. The existing hiring conflict also remains in place: among candidates G and J, no more than `max_one_candidate_g_j` may be brought in at the same time.

Because the team has recently been reorganized, every person who joins must now be brought in with a clearly defined role. Each hired candidate is to serve in exactly one of two roles, `Lead` or `Engineer`; nobody should be left unassigned, and nobody should hold both roles at once. The company also wants the team to have enough senior direction from the start, so at least `min_leads` hires must be placed in the Lead role. Taking a Lead position comes with additional responsibility for technical coordination and planning, so each Lead carries an extra cost of `lead_premium` on top of that candidate’s base `Salary`.

At the same time, management has launched a formal mentorship initiative to speed up onboarding and knowledge transfer inside the group. For each active Lead–Engineer pairing that the team can genuinely support, the company recognizes an operational benefit worth `mentor_bonus`, since those pairings reduce ramp-up friction and improve productivity. This benefit can only be counted where there are both enough Leads and enough Engineers to support it, and the company will count no more than `max_pairs` such pairings. As a result, the hiring plan should be judged on total net cost: base salaries for everyone hired, plus the `lead_premium` for each person assigned as a Lead, offset by `mentor_bonus` for each countable Lead–Engineer mentorship pair.

Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for the specific parameter values, and determine which candidates to hire and how to assign each hire between `Lead` and `Engineer` so that all staffing, experience, budget, and compatibility requirements are satisfied at the lowest possible net hiring cost.
