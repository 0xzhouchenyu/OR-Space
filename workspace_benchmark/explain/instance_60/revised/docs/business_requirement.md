The sales organization no longer wants this territory covered as one continuous run. After several long-day service exceptions, operations moved the plan to two consecutive service days so the representative can reset from the depot between days and keep coverage more dependable. We still have `num_customers = 7` locations in total. Location `1` remains the home base for dispatch and closeout, so any day that is actually used begins at `start_location = 1` and finishes back at `end_location = 1`. The other stops are the customer locations, and the commitment to the market has not changed: every customer location outside the depot must receive one visit during this two-day window, with no duplicate calls.

From a planning perspective, dispatch must first decide which customers belong on day 1 and which belong on day 2. Once a customer is placed on a day, that customer is folded into that day’s driving sequence and nowhere else. For any service day that is opened, the representative leaves location 1, covers that day’s assigned customers in some order, and returns to location 1 as a single coherent trip. Operations will not accept a plan that strands a subset of customers in a disconnected loop away from the depot, because that would not be an executable field route. It is also acceptable, if the economics support it, to leave one of the two days unused; in that case no travel occurs on that day at all.

There is also a standing workload guardrail in the routing policy. The total mileage accumulated within any one day’s trip must remain within `max_route_length_day = 1000`. The cap is intentionally generous for this instance, but it is part of the standard planning template and must still be respected day by day. Travel distances come from `D[i, j]`, using the symmetric mileage table in the data files, with `D[i, i] = 0`. In addition, dispatch treats an opened service day as carrying its own launch cost for preparation, paperwork, and daily setup, so each day that is actually operated brings an extra `route_cost_day = 20` on top of the mileage-driven travel cost.

The planning goal is to find the lowest-cost two-day service plan under these operating rules: cover every customer exactly once across the two days, keep each active day as a depot-to-depot route, respect the daily mileage cap, and avoid paying for an extra operating day unless the distance savings justify it. Please refer to the corresponding CSV files in the data folder for the specific values of `D[i, j]` and the related parameters.
