A textile factory produces two types of fabrics: one for clothing and the other for curtains. The factory operates two shifts, with weekly production time constraints and specific sales requirements for each type of fabric. The factory aims to fully utilize its production time, minimize overtime, and meet sales targets for both types of fabrics.
