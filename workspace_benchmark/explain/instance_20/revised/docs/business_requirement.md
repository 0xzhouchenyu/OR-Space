Bright Future Toys is preparing the production plan for the coming period with a straightforward commercial aim: choose the mix of robots, model cars, building blocks, and dolls that delivers the highest profit, while staying within the period limits on plastic and electronic components recorded in the data files. The product economics and resource consumption should be taken directly from the corresponding CSV parameters.

This period, several non-negotiable operating directives shape what the plant is allowed to run. First, the operations and merchandising teams have confirmed that any plan that includes robots must keep dolls out of the schedule for that same period. Second, the current assortment policy requires model cars to appear only as part of a broader vehicle-and-construction offer, so a plan that includes model cars is acceptable only when building blocks are also present in the period mix. Third, sales has capped dolls relative to model cars, so doll volume for the period may not run ahead of model car volume.

In addition, there is a temporary molding-line restriction that is specific to this planning window and must be treated as binding. The specialized molding setup needed for model cars is the same setup family needed for building blocks, and the plant has only one such setup available across the period. Because the changeover cannot be accommodated within the available window, management has ruled out splitting that setup across both products. In practical terms, once the plan uses that setup for one of those two lines, the other one must be left out for the period.
